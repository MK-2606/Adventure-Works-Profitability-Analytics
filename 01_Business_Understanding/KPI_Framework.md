# KPI Framework
## Adventure Works: Profitability & Commercial Performance Analytics

---

# 1. KPI Philosophy

The objective of this dashboard is not to monitor every available metric.

The objective is to monitor business success through a small set of outcome KPIs and provide structured diagnostic paths that explain KPI movement.

The framework follows a three-layer model:

```text
Outcome Metrics
↓
Diagnostic Metrics
↓
Supporting Dimensions
```

This enables stakeholders to move from business performance monitoring to root-cause investigation.

---

# 2. KPI Selection Framework

| KPI | Why It Was Selected |
|------|---------------------|
| Profit | Primary measure of business value creation. |
| Profit Margin % | Measures revenue quality and profitability efficiency. |
| Revenue Growth % | Measures commercial expansion and growth momentum. |

These KPIs represent stakeholder success and directly support strategic commercial decisions.

# 4. KPI Investigation Framework

| KPI | First Diagnostic Layer | Detailed Investigation |
|------|------------------------|-------------------------|
| Profit | Revenue, Cost, Returns | Product → Category → Region |
| Profit Margin % | Revenue Composition, Cost Structure, Returns | Product → Category → Region |
| Revenue Growth % | Volume, AOV, Contribution, Seasonality | Product → Category → Region → Time |

---

# 5. Stakeholder Investigation Paths

## Profit

```text
Profit
↓
Revenue vs Cost vs Returns
↓
Category
↓
Product
↓
Region
```

Stakeholder Question:

"Is profit changing because we are selling more, spending more, or losing more value through returns?"

---

## Profit Margin %

```text
Profit Margin %
↓
Revenue Composition
Cost Structure
Returns Impact
↓
Category
↓
Product / Region
```

Stakeholder Question:

"Is margin deterioration caused by product mix, pricing, costs, or returns?"

---

## Revenue Growth %

```text
Revenue Growth %
↓
Order Volume
AOV
Contribution Analysis
Seasonality
↓
Category
↓
Product / Region
```

Stakeholder Question:

"Is growth being driven by more orders, larger orders, stronger categories, or stronger regions?"

---

# 6. Framework Outcome

This KPI framework ensures that:

- Business success is measured using outcome KPIs.
- KPI movement can be explained through diagnostic metrics.
- Stakeholders can investigate root causes systematically.
- Dashboard analysis remains aligned with commercial decision-making.
