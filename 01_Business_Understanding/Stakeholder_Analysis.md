# Stakeholder Analysis

## Project

**Adventure Works Profitability & Commercial Performance Analytics**

---

# Stakeholder Identification

The first step in the analytics process was identifying the primary business stakeholder who would consume the dashboard and make decisions based on the analysis.

The Adventure Works dataset contains sales, product, customer, returns, and territory information. Multiple stakeholders could potentially benefit from this data, including:

| Stakeholder Type | Primary Focus |
|------------------|---------------|
| Sales Manager | Revenue growth and sales performance |
| Product Manager | Product performance and category trends |
| Operations Manager | Inventory and operational efficiency |
| Customer Insights Analyst | Customer behavior and segmentation |
| Regional Manager | Territory performance |
| Profitability Analysis Stakeholder | Financial performance and value creation |

After evaluating the available data and potential business outcomes, the project was designed around a **Profitability Analysis Stakeholder**.

---

# Selected Stakeholder

## Profitability Analysis Stakeholder

### Primary Responsibility

Monitor commercial performance and determine whether business growth is translating into sustainable profitability.

### Key Objectives

- Maximize profit generation
- Improve profit margins
- Support revenue growth
- Reduce return-related revenue leakage
- Optimize product portfolio performance
- Improve regional commercial performance
- Support strategic resource allocation

---

# Stakeholder Challenges

Revenue growth alone does not guarantee business success.

A business can generate increasing revenue while simultaneously experiencing:

- Rising costs
- Margin deterioration
- Product profitability issues
- High return rates
- Regional underperformance

The stakeholder therefore requires visibility into both business outcomes and the drivers influencing those outcomes.

---

# Decisions Supported

The dashboard was designed to support the following business decisions:

| Decision Area | Example Decision |
|---------------|------------------|
| Product Strategy | Which products should receive additional investment? |
| Category Strategy | Which categories are driving growth and profitability? |
| Regional Strategy | Which territories deserve additional focus or corrective action? |
| Profitability Management | Where are margins strongest or weakest? |
| Returns Management | Which products or categories require return reduction initiatives? |
| Revenue Growth | Which business areas are driving commercial expansion? |

---

# Information Requirements

To support these decisions, the stakeholder requires visibility into:

## Financial Outcomes

- Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Revenue Growth %

# KPI Framework Alignment

The analytical framework was structured around three outcome KPIs.

## Outcome KPIs

These represent stakeholder success.

- Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Revenue Growth %

These metrics answer:

> How is the business performing?

---

## Diagnostic Metrics

These explain why KPI performance changes.

- Revenue
- Cost
- Orders
- AOV
- ASP
- ARPC
- Return Rate %
- Revenue Contribution %
- Net Revenue
- Mix Shift
- Customer Count

These metrics answer:

> Why is performance changing?

---

## Supporting Metrics

These provide business context.

- Product
- Category
- Region
- Customer Segment
- Month
- Year

These metrics answer:

> Where is performance changing?

---

# Success Criteria

The dashboard is considered successful if the stakeholder can:

- Monitor Profit, Profit Margin %, and Revenue Growth %
- Identify the drivers of business performance
- Detect profitability risks
- Identify return-related revenue leakage
- Compare performance across products, categories, and regions
- Support commercial decision-making using data

---

# Conclusion

The Profitability Analysis Stakeholder was selected because the available data and business objectives aligned most closely with profitability evaluation rather than pure sales reporting.

This stakeholder perspective guided the selection of business questions, KPIs, metrics, dashboard structure, and analytical workflows, ensuring that the final solution supports decision-making rather than simply reporting historical performance.
